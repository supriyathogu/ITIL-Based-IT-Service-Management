This document records practical checks performed during the independent ITSM simulation and distinguishes completed activities from planned validation.

## Testing approach
1. Perform one activity at a time in Jira.
2. Verify the resulting ticket field or status.
3. Capture a screenshot of the result.
4. Record the date, ticket key, and observed outcome.
5. Mark an item complete only when it has been performed and verified.

## Current project outcome
The completed exercise demonstrates basic incident creation, request classification, assignment, an internal note, and a transition to Work in Progress. SLA configuration, dashboard setup, impact/urgency assessment, and technical resolution require separate work and evidence.

## Evidence folder
Store screenshots in the repository's `screenshots/` folder. Use descriptive filenames and avoid exposing credentials, private user data, or confidential organisation information.
