# Priority, Impact, and Urgency

## Definitions
- **Impact:** The extent to which the incident affects users, services, or business operations.
- **Urgency:** How quickly the incident requires attention, considering the consequences of delay.
- **Priority:** The order in which the incident should be handled, often determined using an impact-and-urgency matrix.

## Illustrative matrix
The following matrix is an example for learning. It is not confirmed as the active configuration in the Jira project.

| Impact | Urgency | Illustrative priority |
|---|---|---|
| High | High | Critical |
| High | Medium | High |
| Medium | High | High |
| Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Low | Low | Low |

Organisations may use different priority names, scales, and matrices.

## Sample VPN incident
The Jira ticket displayed **Medium** priority during the exercise. Impact and Urgency displayed as **None** at that point. Therefore, do not claim that Medium was calculated from a completed impact-and-urgency assessment.

## Assessment questions
1. How many users are affected?
2. Is a critical business service unavailable?
3. Is there a workaround?
4. What is the consequence of waiting?
5. Is there a deadline or time-sensitive business activity?

## Configuration evidence
