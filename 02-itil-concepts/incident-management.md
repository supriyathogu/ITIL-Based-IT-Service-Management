# Incident Management

## Definition
Incident Management is the practice of minimising the negative impact of incidents by restoring normal service operation as quickly as practical.

## Typical incident lifecycle
1. **Log:** Record the incident and the user's description.
2. **Categorise:** Select an appropriate category or request type.
3. **Prioritise:** Assess urgency and impact using the organisation's model.
4. **Assign:** Route the ticket to an appropriate support person or team.
5. **Investigate and diagnose:** Gather information and perform approved troubleshooting.
6. **Resolve:** Apply a workaround or permanent fix where possible.
7. **Confirm and close:** Confirm service restoration and complete closure activities.

Actual Jira workflow statuses may differ from this conceptual lifecycle.

## Example
**Incident:** Unable to connect to company VPN.

**Summary:** Unable to connect to company VPN.

**Description:** The VPN connection fails when I try to connect from my laptop.

**Request type used in the exercise:** Report a System Problem.

This is a fictional practice incident. No real VPN fault was reported or investigated.

## Good ticket-writing practices
- Use a short, specific summary.
- Describe the observed behaviour and business effect.
- Include relevant error messages only when they are actually observed.
- Avoid guessing at the root cause.
- Keep internal notes factual and professional.
- Record actions and outcomes in chronological order.
