# ITIL Fundamentals

## What is ITIL?
ITIL is a widely used body of guidance for IT service management. It helps organisations create, deliver, support, and continually improve services that provide value to customers and users.

ITIL 4 describes service management through a service value system and guiding principles. It encourages organisations to consider value, stakeholders, processes, information, technology, and continual improvement together.

## What is IT Service Management?
IT Service Management (ITSM) is the way an organisation designs, delivers, manages, and improves IT services. An IT service desk is commonly a point of contact for users who need help or want to request a service.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Incident | An unplanned interruption to a service or a reduction in service quality |
| Service request | A user request for a standard service or information |
| Problem | A cause, or potential cause, of one or more incidents |
| Change | An addition, modification, or removal that may affect services |
| SLA | An agreement defining service expectations and targets |
| Priority | The relative order in which work should be handled |
| Escalation | Moving an issue to a higher support level or appropriate authority |

## Guiding principles
- Focus on value.
- Start where you are.
- Progress iteratively with feedback.
- Collaborate and promote visibility.
- Think and work holistically.
- Keep it simple and practical.
- Optimise and automate.

## How this project uses ITIL
This project uses sample service desk scenarios to understand incident handling, service requests, problem management, change enablement, and service level management. The documents are learning notes, not a claim of formal organisational ITIL implementation.
