# Problem Management

## Definition
Problem Management reduces the likelihood and impact of incidents by identifying actual and potential causes and managing workarounds and known errors.

## Key terms
- **Problem:** A cause or potential cause of one or more incidents.
- **Root cause:** The underlying reason an incident or problem occurs.
- **Workaround:** A temporary way to reduce or remove the impact when a full resolution is not available.
- **Known error:** A problem that has been analysed but not permanently resolved, with its cause and workaround documented where known.

## Example scenario
If several users repeatedly report VPN connection failures, the service team may raise or investigate a problem to determine whether the incidents share a common cause.

The scenario is illustrative; this project does not claim that a real recurring VPN problem was identified.

## Typical activities
1. Identify recurring or high-impact incidents.
2. Record and assess the problem.
3. Investigate potential causes.
4. Document workarounds and known errors when established.
5. Propose corrective action through the appropriate change process.
6. Review whether recurrence or impact has reduced.
