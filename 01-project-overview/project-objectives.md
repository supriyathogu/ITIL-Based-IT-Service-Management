# Project Objectives

## Purpose
The purpose of this project is to develop foundational, practical understanding of IT Service Management by simulating common service desk activities in Jira Service Management.

## Objectives
1. Understand the role of an IT service desk in supporting users.
2. Learn the difference between incidents and service requests.
3. Explore Jira Service Management project setup and request types.
4. Create, classify, assign, and update sample tickets.
5. Understand the relationship between Impact, Urgency, and Priority.
6. Practise ticket workflow transitions and internal documentation.
7. Learn the purpose of SLAs and escalation procedures.
8. Document sample service desk records and validation evidence.

## Expected learning outcomes
After completing the planned activities, the learner should be able to:
- Explain a basic incident lifecycle.
- Describe how a request enters a service desk.
- Identify the purpose of ticket fields and statuses.
- Explain how a support team may use priority and SLAs.
- Maintain clear ticket notes and project documentation.

## Success criteria
- A Jira Service Management project is accessible.
- At least one sample incident is created and its ticket key recorded.
- Assignment and a workflow transition are demonstrated.
- Documentation and CSV templates are maintained.
- Screenshots are added only when the corresponding activity is actually completed.
